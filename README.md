# Secondhand

A one-dollar cartridge from a yard sale. No box, no manual. Someone wrote the name on the label in marker.

It looks like an old top-down adventure: a little village, a broken bell on a lake, three pieces to find. But save file 1 still belongs to the last player, Wren. And the longer you play, the more the game seems to know about you. The villagers start saying your name. The statue in the square isn't on its plinth anymore. The music slows down, then plays backwards.

- Play in your browser: https://buggygamesyt.github.io/secondhand/
- Android: grab `Secondhand.apk` from the latest release.

Controls: arrows / WASD walk, Z / Space / Enter = A (talk, read, swing your stick), X / Esc = B (skip text, pause), M mute. On a phone: slide your thumb on the pad, tap A and B.

All story, characters, art, and music are original.
